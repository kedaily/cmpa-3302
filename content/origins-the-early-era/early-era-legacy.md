# Early Era Legacy

The pre-1980s period established the core ideas, technologies, and business models that still shape video games today.

## Technological Foundations

- Real-time interactive graphics on electronic displays
- Dedicated hardware for games (rather than general-purpose computers only)
- Controller interfaces (knobs, paddles, joysticks)
- The concept of a “console” that connects to a television

These ideas moved from one-off laboratory machines to mass-produced consumer products.

## Business and Cultural Impact

| Contribution | Early Example | Later Influence |
|--------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Coin-operated play | *Pong* cabinets | Modern arcade and location-based entertainment |
| Home console model | Magnavox Odyssey | Every subsequent home system |
| Cartridge / expandable library | Atari 2600 | Software-driven hardware platforms |
| Third-party development | Activision (1979) | Independent game development |
