# Origins & The Early Era

Before video games became a global industry, they were experiments, prototypes, and one-off machines built in laboratories and garages. This category traces that foundational period- from the first interactive electronic displays in the 1940s to the commercial breakthroughs of the early 1970s that made video games a consumer product.

The early era ends a cartridge-based consoles and the arcade golden age begin to dominate in the late 1970s.

## Quick Timeline

- **1947** - First interactive electronic game (CRT Amusement Device)
- **1958** - *Tennis for Two*
- **1962** - *Spacewars!*
- **1971** - *Computer Space* (first commercial arcade game)
- **1972** - Magnavox Odyssey + *Pong*
- **1977** - Atari 2600
